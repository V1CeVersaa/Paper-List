Synthesis pages are where cross-paper and cross-topic conclusions should be written back into the repository.

Use this directory for:

- structured comparisons across multiple papers
- survey-like summaries inside one topic
- cross-topic links that do not belong to a single paper note
- consolidated answers that should not remain trapped in chat
