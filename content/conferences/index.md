Conference pages record what feels new at a specific venue, especially signals that do not yet fit cleanly into an existing topic page.

Typical focus:

- new directions that started to cohere across multiple papers
- methods or problem framings that suddenly became visible at workshop or oral level
- recurring experimental patterns worth watching in the next cycle
- notes from workshop talks, invited talks, and oral sessions that affect how topics should be expanded later

Suggested page pattern:

- `content/conferences/<venue-year>.md`
- capture `Emerging Directions`, `New Methods`, `Workshop Notes`, `Oral Notes`, and `Follow-up Intakes`
- link out to affected `topics/`, `syntheses/`, and later paper notes when they are read
